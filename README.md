# WASAPhoto

## Overview
Keep in touch with your friends by sharing photos of special moments, thanks to WASAPhoto! You can upload your photos directly from your PC, and they will be visible to everyone following you.

## Features
- **Profile**: Create you own profile and personalize it on WASAPhoto.
- **Photo management**: Upload/remove any photo on your profile.
- **User interaction**: Search for other users, following them, banning who's unliked, liking other photos.
- **User-friendly interface**: Simple and clean UI, easy to use and easy to understand.
